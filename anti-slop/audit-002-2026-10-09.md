## Delivery gate

- Purpose: PASS. Teacher preparation, passage reading, correction, and results retain their existing functions. Paper colors, serif passage type, chapter dividers, and ruled metrics support the notebook direction. No decorative assets, gradients, new icons, or invented claims were added. Shadows are limited to the floating word editor.
- Layout: PASS. Preparation uses a two-column spread that stacks on phones. Reading remains continuous; results use a ruled summary. Desktop and 320-pixel phone screenshots are saved beside this report.
- Accessibility: PASS for inspected controls. Keyboard activation opens the word editor; Escape returns focus to its passage word. Word targets are at least 44 pixels wide and 65 pixels high in the tested 320-pixel layout. The editor fits a 390-pixel screen. Setup and results have no horizontal overflow at 320 pixels.
- Contrast: PASS. Lowest measured text contrast is 4.57:1. Muted text on paper is 6.26:1; rust actions are 7.40:1 with white labels; instructional amber is 6.15:1. Placeholder text uses the measured muted color.
- States: PASS. Loading, empty, unavailable-score, recent-results failure, save retry, and stale-response handling are covered by automated checks. Browser checks exercised reading recovery and all five word correction actions with simulated readings.
- Content and motion: PASS. No em dashes, fake testimonials, invented statistics, remote fonts, new navigation, or unnecessary animation were introduced. The fixed light paper appearance is intentional and documented.
- Functional checks: PASS within the stated scope. The actual local app served setup, passage, engine, and result endpoints successfully. Missing models correctly disable Start. A temporary browser fixture exercised reading, results, correction, saving, CSV download, and restart without creating learner records in the app.
- Automated verification: 65 tests passed, with 3 subtests passed. Browser regression checks passed. `git diff --check` is clean.

## Limits

Live microphone capture and speech recognition were not exercised because local speech models are missing. Simulated browser readings verify the interface flows, not recognition accuracy. The saved previews show the actual app, without fixture controls.

No new actionable design findings remain in this audit.
