About Booklat

From passage to report

Pick a passage. The child reads aloud. Tap words to correct suggested marks. Save the reading offline and export the scores as CSV.

On your laptop

After setup, Booklat works without internet. Its local server listens only on 127.0.0.1. Voice recording is optional and saved in the local database when enabled.

Voice recordings stay on this computer. Microphone checks are not recorded.
