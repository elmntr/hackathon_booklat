# Booklat reading notebook

Direction selected by the owner: warm paper colors and book-style typography.

Reading this as a reading notebook for teachers beside a learner, using a printed-book visual language.

Dial: ENERGY 2 / RHYTHM 2 / MOTION 1.

- Warm cream surfaces recall a notebook and keep a light reading environment.
- Dark brown ink gives passage text the contrast of printed pages.
- Rust marks the primary action; semantic green, red, and amber retain their reading-assessment meanings.
- Georgia and system serif fallbacks give headings and passages a book voice without downloading fonts.
- System sans-serif keeps labels, controls, and recognition statuses easy to scan.
- The setup screen pairs the teacher's controls with an open passage; mobile stacks them in reading order.
- Straight paper edges and chapter-like horizontal dividers separate preparation, passage, and results.
- Result metrics share a single ruled summary instead of four floating cards.
- Wide passage margins and generous line spacing leave room for word marks and teacher corrections.
- Shadows appear only on temporary editing controls to distinguish their position above the page.
- Motion is limited to native interaction and hover feedback so the learner's text stays still.
- A fixed light paper theme serves the selected notebook direction; no theme toggle is introduced.
- No new logo, illustration, external font, or decorative image is needed.
